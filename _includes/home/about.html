I develop foundation models that connect Earth observation, multimodal intelligence, and scientific discovery.

I am a Postdoctoral Research Scientist at Singapore Management University, advised by Prof. Qianru Sun. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Nankai University in 2026, advised by Professors Jian Yang, Xiang Li, and Ming-Ming Cheng. Before that, I completed a four-year integrated M.Eng. in Computer Science at University College London with First-Class Honours.

My work spans multi-modal remote sensing perception, efficient specialization and continual learning of multimodal large language models (MLLMs), and self-evolving research agents for Earth and environmental science. I welcome research collaborations; please get in touch by email.
